# What happened

On 2026-11-20 the product owner asked one lead to bring back the sidebar's lines. By the source's report, the same
morning the product owner asked the owner of the Desktop rework the same thing by a direct message, and two builds
started about an hour apart: the lead's local branch, and a developer slice of the rework. The overlap surfaced only
because the rework's lead wrote first. The product owner then picked one builder, and the other slice stopped and
handed over its branch; oats-project/oats#2011's description records that the rework's lead was told and stopped
its slice.

# How to apply

At the start of a task that touches code another person's effort is changing, message that effort's lead once,
quoting the request, and ask whether it already holds the same ask. Settle one builder before either side builds.

This is the across-people case of a lesson the lanes already hold: resolve who builds before launch, not after two
builds have started ([the release lane and project lanes](/nodes/oats-maintainer/stewardship/release-lane-and-project-lanes.md)).
There a lane map decides; here two people's efforts share no map, so the check is one message to the other effort's
lead.

Evidence: OKF proposal from oats-engineering-expert/sidebar-collapsed-sibling-lines-lead, 2026-11-20.
