# The decision

In template-v4.0.0 of the engineering coordination template, what every
coordinating role must hold goes once into the "house rules" of the capability
every such soul composes (the landing rules), as an always-on line, with its
definition in that capability's detail skill. Other texts point at it and add
only what is theirs (for example the order of the check when picking a
child's model). A soul that composes nothing shared (the code reviewer) keeps
its own short sentence, and the adapting guide says so.

# Why

Three briefings each carried the hosts rule with its own adapt marker, two
carried the quality floor's marker, and the model floor was stated in four
places. Each release had to change every copy, and one copy was missed twice
(the reviewer's, which kept a relative wording of the floor; see
[the limit lesson](/claims/lessons/a-limit-in-coordination-texts-is-checked-first-and-binds-the-value-used.md)).
With one home, an adapt marker exists once per question: the template went
from 25 markers to 21.

Rejected: a separate "house rules" capability (one more id to copy and compose
for five sentences); leaving each briefing its copy (the drift above). The
lead's call within the effort's delegated design authority.
